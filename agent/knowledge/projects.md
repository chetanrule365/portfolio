# Personal / side projects

## VyaparTez — https://vyapartez.com
- Stack: React, TypeScript, Cloudflare
- Online store builder for Indian businesses: branded storefronts, catalogs, cart, coupons, orders, Cashfree/Razorpay payments, merchant billing on Cloudflare.
- Why it matters: real Indian merchants, payments, and ownership of a product surface end-to-end.

## Options Paper Lab — https://project-one.up.railway.app/demo
- Stack: React, Node.js, MongoDB
- Paper-trading lab for Nifty, Bank Nifty, Sensex: live quotes, strategy playbook, simulated fills, backtests on Dhan data stored in MongoDB.
- Why it matters: learning markets systems by building the loop (quotes → strategy → fill → backtest), not just charts.

## Expense Tracker — https://chetan-et.vercel.app
- Stack: Next.js, TypeScript, Firebase
- Mobile expense app: daily spends, categories, cards, monthly limits; Firebase sync; receipt import from photo/PDF.
- Why it matters: practical mobile UX + sync + document import — small product, full stack ownership.
